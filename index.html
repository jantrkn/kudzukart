03 // Origin & Craft

Pedal-Powered Comfort Food for the Front Range.

Named after the resilient, fast-growing vine from Japan that was imported to the American South and rooted in the Japanese tradition of portable rice balls, Kudzu Kart started with a simple blueprint: build an ultra-low-footprint street food cart towed entirely by an electric cargo bike in Englewood, Colorado.

Instead of generators, propane tanks, and idling trucks, we use high-efficiency insulated coolers and pedal-assisted e-bikes to bring freshly pressed onigiri directly to neighborhood markets, parks, and taprooms.

Because Celiac-safe, plant-based street food shouldn't feel like an afterthought, our entire prep commissary is 100% gluten-free and 100% plant-based—zero cross-contamination, just crisp nori, seasoned short-grain rice, and bold, nostalgic fillings.
